Come in.

Normal stands for the quiet majority of our society: the many currently disengaged from the greater community, who feel paralysed, or uninvited.

Consider this your invitation.

We believe progress itself demands that humans find better ways to love and serve one another. This house is where we're trying.

1 a laboratory

Kitchen

A place for experimentation and adventure. A place to challenge the status quo with unabashed authenticity, boldness and openness to being different, where trying is celebrated and failure isn't feared.

Because seeking a better future should feel less like a dream and more like an exploration in motion.

Principle

Adventure Fear

We explore ordinary life with fun, risk, boldness and openness to the unexpected, and keep a safe place for experimentation, failure and unabashed authenticity.

How we build

Courage to explore

Offering different perspectives and challenging the status quo. Trying new things, even into uncharted territory. We don't have to solve everything at once; we create something different and make whatever impact we can.

↑ back to the plan

2 a seat at the table

Living room

A space where every individual is considered and has a seat at the table. Where every person feels recognised for who they are, and heard even in a room full of noise.

Principle

Considered Dismissed

We champion a culture of care for our fellow humans, where every individual is considered and left a seat at the table.

How we build

Authenticity & intention

Care, intention and precision in everything we do. Beyond novelty and hype, value is what keeps people coming back. We build for depth, centred on each individual we serve, not just "the audience".

↑ back to the plan

3 a shelter

Bedroom

Where space is held for real rest. Not just your recommended eight hours of sleep, but time to reconnect with ourselves, away from the constant rush and structure of everyday life.

A place where you feel happy, and in touch with the things you love.

Principle

Dwelling Rushing

Within the constant momentum of school, work and structure, we hold space to disconnect, and to reconnect with ourselves.

↑ back to the plan

4 a living library

Library

In the end, Normal hopes to be a living library filled with pieces of a better society, each one individually curated, and demonstrated rather than merely described.

A tangible glimpse of what could be normal, through our activations, cultural works and gatherings.

On the shelves

How we build

Staying curious

Our drive and sense of ownership come from a never-ceasing excitement and passion.

garden

Principle

Flourish Bare survival

We want people to have the space, time and stability to be whole, and to reach a fullness where they feel in touch with themselves. It's the foundation that future growth is built on.

Principle

Fulfilled in pursuit of purpose

We want Normal to feel like a warm ray of sunshine after a cold day of thunderstorms. A quiet warmth you can't get enough of, that you come back to again and again, and that helps you find your purpose.
