This Data Protection Notice ("Notice") sets out the basis on which New Normal [full legal name] ("New Normal", "we", "us" or "our") may collect, use, disclose or otherwise process the personal data of our employees, interns and other staff, job applicants, [customers, partners and visitors: to be confirmed], and anyone else whose personal data we handle, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 ("PDPA"). It covers all our activities, including Hearth, the internal work suite our staff use for chat, voice rooms, meetings, calendar, tasks and documents. This Notice applies to personal data in our possession or under our control, including personal data held for us by organisations we have engaged to process it.

This Notice applies to staff based or studying outside Singapore, and to former staff and others whose data we still keep. Where we collect personal data for a particular activity, we may give you a further notice for it, which applies alongside this one.

Personal data clauses 1–3

1. As used in this Notice, "personal data" means data, whether true or not, about an individual (whether a current, former or prospective member of staff, a job applicant, [a customer] or otherwise) who can be identified: (a) from that data; or (b) from that data and other information to which we have or are likely to have access.

2. Depending on the nature of your relationship with us, some examples of personal data which we may collect include your name, identification numbers such as NRIC, FIN or work pass details where the law requires or permits it, contact information such as residential address, email address or telephone number, nationality, gender, date of birth, employment and education history, bank account details for payroll, [medical information such as medical certificates], photographs and other audio-visual recordings, and records of your use of our systems, including Hearth (clause 8). [DPO to confirm this list against New Normal's records.]

3. Other terms used in this Notice shall have the meanings given to them in the PDPA (where the context so permits).

Collection, use and disclosure of personal data clauses 4–7

4. We generally do not collect your personal data unless (a) it is provided to us by you directly, or by a third party you have authorised to give it to us (your "authorised representative"), after you have been told the purposes for which it is collected and, where we rely on your consent, have given it; or (b) collection and use without consent is permitted or required by the PDPA or other laws, for example to manage your employment with us. We will not use your personal data for a purpose that has not been notified to you, except where the law permits or requires it.

5. We may collect and use your personal data for any or all of the following purposes:

  • establishing or managing your relationship with us. This includes:
    • where you are a member of staff (including interns and staff based or studying overseas):
      • recruiting and onboarding you, and managing your employment, including payroll, benefits, leave, performance and conduct [DPO to confirm]; and
      • providing you with Hearth and our other work tools, as set out in clauses 8 to 13;
    • where you are a job applicant, assessing your application and your suitability for the role; and
    • where you are a [customer: to be confirmed], providing the products and services you ask for;
  • verifying your identity;
  • responding to, handling and processing queries, requests, applications, complaints and feedback from you;
  • keeping our systems and data secure, including keeping audit logs, investigating incidents and applying legal holds;
  • complying with any applicable laws, regulations, codes of practice, guidelines or rules, or assisting in law enforcement and investigations conducted by any governmental or regulatory authority;
  • any other purposes for which you have provided the information; and
  • any other incidental purposes related to or in connection with the above.

6. We do not rely on your consent for most of these purposes, so that records we must keep are not lost if someone withdraws consent. [Proposed, to be confirmed by the DPO and counsel:]

  • we rely on the PDPA exception for managing the employment relationship for managing your employment, providing Hearth, managing your Hearth account and recording scheduled meetings and calls in Hearth;
  • we rely on legitimate interests for keeping our systems secure and, in Hearth, for recording drop-in rooms, showing recordings to people who were not there, activity insights and the Org Brain. We assess, before each purpose starts, that our interests outweigh any adverse effect on you, and you may ask our Data Protection Officer for information about that assessment; and
  • we rely on your consent for the Hearth transcription test set [and any other purpose that needs consent, such as marketing: to be confirmed].

7. We may disclose your personal data:

  • where such disclosure is required for, or in connection with, the services you asked us for;
  • to colleagues who need it for their work and, in Hearth, in line with clause 11;
  • to third party service providers, agents and other organisations we have engaged to perform any of the purposes in clause 5 for us, including those listed in clause 33; and
  • to comply with any applicable laws, regulations, codes of practice, guidelines, rules or requests by public agencies, or to assist in law enforcement and investigations.

We do not sell your personal data, and our providers may not use it to train AI models.

Hearth, our internal work suite clauses 8–13

8. Hearth is the work suite our staff use for chat, voice rooms, meetings, calendar, tasks and documents. Clauses 8 to 13 apply to everyone who uses it, and to people who do not use it but are named in it or picked up in a recording. An administrator adds you to Hearth from our staff records, and Hearth then collects, depending on how you use it:

  • account and profile: your name, mobile phone number, department, team(s), manager and role, and the photo, title, working hours, time zone and status you add;
  • sign-in and devices: one-time codes sent by SMS, your PIN (stored only in a protected, hashed form), passkeys, the devices you sign in on and your sign-in records;
  • what you create and share: messages, direct messages, voice messages, files, documents, sheets, slide decks, tasks, calendar events, comments and reactions;
  • recordings: the audio of every voice session with two or more people, including drop-in rooms and direct calls, and video and screen share when they are switched on, together with any slides presented;
  • activity: presence (whether you are online or in a room), read receipts, who viewed a recording, transcript or notes, counts and durations used for activity insights (such as messages sent and time spent talking in rooms), and security and audit logs; and
  • your acknowledgement of this Notice, with its version, date and time.

9. Hearth does not ask for your NRIC, FIN or other identification numbers. Please do not post them, passwords or similar sensitive data in Hearth; Hearth may warn you if it detects them.

10. In Hearth, we use your personal data for:

  • providing Hearth to you. This includes hosting and delivering your messages, voice messages, files, documents, sheets, slide decks, tasks and calendar events, and sending notifications to your devices;
  • managing your Hearth account. This includes signing you in, verifying your identity when your PIN is reset or your phone number changes, and removing your access when you leave;
  • recording and keeping a record of meetings and calls. This includes:
    • recording scheduled meetings and calls;
    • recording drop-in room sessions;
    • keeping the slides presented in a session with its record; and
    • showing a session's recording to the audience set out in clause 11, including people who were not there;
  • activity insights [when switched on]. These are counts and durations only (messages sent, reactions, time in rooms and meetings, talk time and tasks completed). You see your own figures; Owners, Admins and the Leadership role see figures across New Normal. They never score what you say, and a person checks them against the underlying activity before they are used in any decision about you;
  • AI features [when switched on, see clause 13]. These are transcribing sessions and voice messages, meeting notes, the Org Brain, your personal assistant, specialist agents, and built-in summaries and writing help. Each uses only content you could open yourself; specialist agents never read direct messages; and restricted rooms are never indexed; and
  • improving transcription quality, using a test set of clips recorded specifically for it, only with each speaker's separate consent.

11. Who can see what in Hearth:

  • every room and call shows a recording indicator and plays a join tone, and there is no option to opt out of recording;
  • a session in a channel's room can be seen by the members of that channel [or, for a public room, everyone at New Normal: to be confirmed];
  • a scheduled meeting can be seen by its invitees and participants, and a direct or group call by its participants;
  • a session in a restricted room (for example HR matters or performance 1:1s) can be seen by its participants only;
  • direct messages are private to the people in them; and
  • participants can see who has viewed a session's recording, transcript or notes.

12. Our Data Protection Officer and Compliance Officers may open Hearth content they could not otherwise see, including direct messages and restricted sessions, only to handle a logged case (such as your access or correction request, an incident or an investigation) and only with a stated reason. Every item they open is logged, and this access is never available to any AI feature. [Whether participants are told about such access is still to be decided.]

13. When we switch on Hearth's AI features, we will tell you at least fourteen (14) days beforehand and ask you to acknowledge an updated Notice. From then on Hearth will also process: transcripts of recorded sessions and voice messages; AI-generated meeting notes and action items; your conversations with your personal assistant and our specialist agents, and anything you ask your assistant to remember; and a searchable index of content (the "Org Brain") that lets the AI find what you are allowed to see.

Withdrawing your consent clauses 14–17

14. The consent that you provide for the collection, use and disclosure of your personal data will remain valid until such time it is withdrawn by you or your authorised representative. You may withdraw your consent, or object to any of the processing in this Notice, by email or otherwise in writing to our Data Protection Officer at the contact details in clause 36, or, if you use Hearth, through the request form in Hearth's Settings. If you are unable to submit your request in writing, or need help with it, you can ask to speak to or meet with our Data Protection Officer.

15. On receiving your request, we may need reasonable time (depending on its complexity and its impact on your relationship with us) to process it and to tell you the consequences of acceding to it, including any legal consequences that may affect your rights and liabilities to us. In general, we shall seek to process your request within ten (10) business days of receiving it.

16. Whilst we respect your decision to withdraw your consent, depending on the nature and scope of your request, we may not be in a position to continue providing some services to you, and we shall tell you so before completing your request. Withdrawing consent does not stop processing that relies on a PDPA exception rather than on consent (clause 6), such as recording work meetings in Hearth, and does not affect our right to continue to collect, use and disclose personal data where the PDPA or other laws permit or require it without consent. If you withdraw consent to the Hearth transcription test set, we delete your clips from it.

17. Should you decide to cancel your withdrawal of consent, please inform us in the manner described in clause 14.

Access to and correction of personal data clauses 18–23

18. If you wish to make (a) an access request for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or for information about the ways in which we have used or disclosed it in the past year, or (b) a correction request to correct or update any personal data we hold about you, you may submit your request by email or otherwise in writing to our Data Protection Officer at the contact details in clause 36, or, if you use Hearth, through the request form in Hearth's Settings. If you need help with your request, you can ask to speak to or meet with our Data Protection Officer.

19. An access request covers everything we hold about you, including, in Hearth, what you sent, spoke or created, and what others sent, said or wrote that names or concerns you, including direct messages and restricted sessions. Before we release anything, our Data Protection Officer reviews each item and removes or withholds other people's personal data, as the PDPA requires.

20. [To be decided by the DPO: whether a fee applies.] A reasonable fee may be charged for an access request. If so, we will tell you the fee before processing your request.

21. You may ask us to correct personal data about you even in items you cannot open yourself. Where we correct a Hearth transcript or AI note, we keep the original machine output alongside the correction. If we decide not to make a correction, we will record what you asked for alongside the item.

22. We will respond to your request as soon as reasonably possible. Should we not be able to respond within thirty (30) days after receiving your request, we will tell you in writing within those thirty (30) days when we will be able to respond. If we are unable to give you any personal data or to make a correction you requested, we shall generally tell you why (except where the PDPA does not require us to).

23. If you have left us, you may still make any request in this Notice through our Data Protection Officer. We will verify your identity without relying on your old Hearth phone number or PIN.

Protection of personal data clauses 24–26

24. To safeguard your personal data from unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal or similar risks, we have put in place administrative, physical and technical measures. [New Normal's measures outside Hearth, DPO to confirm: for example minimised collection of personal data, access on a need-to-know basis, encryption, up-to-date antivirus protection, regular patching, secure erasure of storage media before disposal, and multi-factor authentication.] In Hearth, our measures include:

  • storing Hearth's data in Singapore, encrypted in transit and at rest, with separate encryption keys for each kind of data;
  • signing in with a one-time SMS code plus your PIN or a passkey on every new device, and re-authentication for every administrator action;
  • checking, on every request, that you may open what you ask for, so that no screen or AI feature shows you anything you could not open yourself;
  • restricted rooms for sensitive conversations, and logged, case-bound access for our Data Protection Officer and Compliance Officers (clause 12);
  • audit logs that cannot be edited or deleted by the app;
  • encrypting copies of content kept on your device for offline use, and wiping them when you sign out or are signed out remotely;
  • written agreements with our service providers (clause 34); and
  • an independent penetration test before Hearth is launched across New Normal, and regular security reviews after that.

25. You should be aware, however, that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. While security cannot be guaranteed, we strive to protect your information and keep reviewing and improving our security measures. If a data breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the PDPA requires.

26. If your phone is lost or stolen, please tell a Hearth administrator at once, so they can sign you out of your devices.

Accuracy of personal data clauses 27–28

27. We generally rely on personal data provided by you (or your authorised representative). In order to ensure that your personal data is current, complete and accurate, please update us if there are changes to your personal data by informing our Data Protection Officer at the contact details in clause 36, or, in Hearth, by updating your profile.

28. Hearth transcripts and AI notes [when switched on] are machine-generated and may contain errors. They are labelled as machine-generated, participants can correct them, and anyone who exports one or uses it in a decision about you is asked to confirm it was checked against the recording first.

Retention of personal data clauses 29–32

29. We may retain your personal data for as long as it is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required or permitted by applicable laws, such as the periods for which employment, accounting and tax records must be kept. [Retention periods for New Normal's records outside Hearth: DPO to set.]

30. Hearth deletes each kind of data automatically at the end of its period, together with everything derived from it, and logs each deletion. A legal hold or an open access request pauses deletion. Hearth's periods are [proposed, to be confirmed by the DPO, except the two audio periods, which are decided]:

  • session audio and video: 120 days;
  • voice-message audio: 360 days;
  • messages, transcripts, AI notes and voice-message transcripts: 3 years;
  • documents, sheets, slide decks and files: 3 years after they were last edited, unless the owner renews them [or a longer period the law requires for that kind of record];
  • items in the Drive's Trash: 30 days after they are trashed;
  • tasks and calendar events: 3 years;
  • conversations with your assistant, agents and built-in AI: 1 year;
  • activity-insight figures: 2 years;
  • audit and access logs: 2 years;
  • backups: 35 days; and
  • your acknowledgements of this Notice: as long as we keep any of your other personal data.

31. When you leave us, your Hearth access ends everywhere within a minute. The work content you created is kept for the periods above, and ownership of your documents, files and channels passes to your manager or team. Your personal assistant's memory is deleted at once. Your phone number, devices and sign-in records are deleted with the audit logs after 2 years, and your Hearth profile is then reduced to your name and title, so your past work stays attributed to you.

32. We will cease to retain your personal data, or remove the means by which the data can be associated with you, as soon as it is reasonable to assume that such retention no longer serves the purpose for which the personal data was collected, and is no longer necessary for legal or business purposes.

Transfers of personal data outside of Singapore clauses 33–35

33. We store Hearth's data in Singapore, with Amazon Web Services (AWS) in its Singapore region. [New Normal's other systems and providers that hold personal data outside Singapore: DPO to list.] Some Hearth service providers process parts of its data outside Singapore:

  • LiveKit Cloud carries voice and video in rooms through Singapore, but keeps connection logs and room details such as participants' names in [the United States or the European Union: to be confirmed];
  • push notification services run by Apple, Google, Mozilla and Microsoft receive your device's notification address and an identifier telling Hearth what to fetch, not the content of your messages, and process them [mainly in the United States];
  • Hearth's SMS provider for sign-in codes is AWS in Singapore [with Twilio, in the United States, as a fallback, if we use it]; and
  • when Hearth's AI features are switched on (clause 13), OpenAI transcribes the spoken parts of recordings and voice messages outside Singapore, in [countries to be confirmed in writing by OpenAI]. Hearth's other AI processing uses Claude through AWS in Singapore and does not leave Singapore.

34. Before any provider receives your personal data, we sign a written agreement that requires a standard of protection at least comparable to the PDPA, names the countries where your data is processed, and requires the provider to tell us promptly of any incident. Our providers may not use your personal data to train AI models, and we ask them not to keep it where they offer that. We keep a register of these providers, which our Data Protection Officer reviews, and update this Notice when it changes.

35. If you are based or studying outside Singapore, your personal data is transferred to Singapore when you work with us or use Hearth. The laws of the country you are in may give you further rights, and may limit how recordings can be made there. Please tell our Data Protection Officer where you are based, and contact them about any such rights.
