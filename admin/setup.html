your admin login

Pick a name and password. This page scrambles the password in your browser (nothing is sent anywhere) and gives you one line to pass to whoever runs Normal's Vercel project.

your login line

  1. Send this line to whoever runs the Vercel project (it's safe to send: it can't be turned back into your password).
  2. In Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables, they add it to ADMIN_USERS. Several people go on separate lines, or separated by commas.
  3. After the next deploy, sign in at admin.newnormal.sg with your name and password.
