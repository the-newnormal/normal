First Master Admin login

Only for the first Master Admin or two. Everyone else gets a login from the portal (accounts → create account). This page scrambles your password in your browser (nothing is sent) and gives you one line for Vercel.

  1. In Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables, add this line to ADMIN_USERS. It's safe to share: it can't be turned back into your password.
  2. Redeploy, then sign in at admin.newnormal.sg with this username and password.
